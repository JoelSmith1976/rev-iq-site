About Rev-IQ

Commercial thinking, built from actually running the function.

Rev-IQ exists because most software businesses solve product problems well and commercial problems badly — not from a lack of intelligence, but from a lack of structure.

What Rev-IQ is

Rev-IQ is a resource hub and advisory practice focused on growth, retention and commercial operating performance for SaaS, software, technology and AI-built businesses.

The frameworks, worksheets and advisory pathways here are built on hands-on experience leading commercial functions — sales, marketing, customer success, and revenue operations — inside SaaS and enterprise software businesses operating across Australia, New Zealand and the UK, including at CRO and Group CEO level.

That experience shows up as practical structure, not theory: operating rhythms that actually get used, pricing models that survive contact with a sales team, and onboarding plans that don't depend on one specific person being in the room.

Why Rev-IQ exists

Most commercial advice aimed at software founders is either too generic to apply, or sold as a program before anyone has diagnosed the actual problem.

Rev-IQ starts from the diagnosis — free frameworks first, direct support only where it's genuinely useful.

How Rev-IQ works

Four principles run through everything published or delivered here.

  • 01

    Useful before it's promotional

    Every resource has to stand on its own — usable without ever talking to Rev-IQ directly.

  • 02

    Structure over personality

    The emphasis is on frameworks and grounded observations, not on building a following around an individual.

  • 03

    Diagnosis before intervention

    Advisory and fractional work always starts by identifying where the constraint actually is — not by proposing a standard package.

  • 04

    Built for operators, by an operator

    Every framework has been tested against the reality of running a commercial function, not just written about it.
