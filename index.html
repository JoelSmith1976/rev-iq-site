About Rev-IQ

Rev-IQ is intended to be more than a website. It is a platform for publishing practical, experience-shaped thinking on the conditions that support healthy growth, stronger retention and more effective operating performance.

The tone is deliberate: useful rather than noisy, clear rather than performative, and grounded in the belief that good thinking should help leaders make better decisions. The site will evolve gradually as more essays, resources and operating perspectives are added.

Approach

Rev-IQ is designed to foreground useful ideas over personality. The emphasis is on practical frameworks, grounded observations and thinking that helps leaders make better decisions.
