Who it's for

Founders who still run sales, heads of sales and revenue operations leads in B2B technology businesses who don't trust their pipeline or forecast. It also suits CEOs and board members who want to sanity-check a forecast before relying on it.

What it does

Checks every open deal against time-in-stage limits, next steps, close dates and required fields, works out the trusted pipeline, lists the deals to review this week, and gives you binary stage definitions, CRM fixes and a five-minute weekly routine.

Built on

Rev-IQ's framework Fixing the Pipeline Mess So You Can Forecast With Confidence, and its CRM Pipeline Configuration Checklist.

You'll need

A CSV or Excel export from your CRM of open deals, plus deals closed in the last three to six months. Stage, value, owner, close date, next step and stage entry date help most. Remove contact details first. Not for: Writing sales emails, coaching individual deals or building a forecast from scratch.

How it works

What happens when you run it

  1. Export your deals from HubSpot, Salesforce, Pipedrive, Zoho or any other CRM.
  2. Confirm your stages and how many days a deal should normally sit in each.
  3. A built-in checker reads the export and applies the same tests to every deal.
  4. You get the trusted pipeline, the deals to review this week and the fixes to make once.

Try asking: "Health check our pipeline from this CRM export."

It runs inside your own AI account. Nothing you enter is sent to Rev-IQ.

Where it's breaking

Six deals are past their stage limit, three have close dates in the past and four have no next step or an overdue one. One lost deal has no reason recorded.

The forecast hangs on one deal

Riverside Hospitality (A$108,000, 29% of open value) has sat in Commercials in Review for 71 days against a 14-day limit, with no next step and a close date that passed on 30 September. Contact, re-date or disqualify it this week.

Northwind Rostering is a fictional 30-customer software business used to test every Rev-IQ skill. Your output will reflect your own information.
