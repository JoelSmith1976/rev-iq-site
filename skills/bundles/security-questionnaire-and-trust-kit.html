Who it's for

Built for the work, not the theory

Founders, revenue and operations leaders of B2B technology businesses, roughly 5 to 150 people, whose deals slow down in procurement or who are facing their first security questionnaires or investor diligence. It suits teams without a dedicated security lead.

You'll need: The questionnaire, plus confirmed facts from whoever knows: your systems and who has access, devices, backups, hosting, vendors and any security documents. The skills never fill gaps with assumptions.

Not for: Security audits, penetration testing, certification such as SOC 2 or ISO 27001, legal advice on privacy or breach notification, or regulated industries with specific security standards.

Built on: The Minimum Viable Security Posture Every Technology Business Needs, How to Stop Losing Weeks to Customer Security Questionnaires, Why Access Control Is the Cheapest Risk You’re Not Managing, You Can’t Protect Data You Haven’t Classified, Your Vendor Stack Is Your Risk Surface: Treat It That Way, What to Actually Do in the First Hour of a Security Incident.

Buying the Security Questionnaire and Trust Kit

What do I get when I buy?

An instant download of every skill in the bundle for your chosen AI platform, as one .zip or skill by skill, plus a step-by-step install guide. Your download page stays available for 12 months, and includes any updated versions released in that time. You can switch platform on the download page, so you're not locked into the one you picked at checkout.

Is the price inclusive of GST?

Yes. Prices are in Australian dollars and include GST where it applies, so the price you see is the price you pay. If you tick "I'm purchasing as a business" at checkout and enter your ABN, Stripe treats it as a business purchase and no GST is charged, but the price stays the same. Stripe emails your receipt.

Can I get a refund?

No. Because the skills are digital files delivered straight away, paid skills aren't refundable for change of mind, buying the wrong product or because your AI platform or plan doesn't support skills. Please check the platform requirements on this page before you buy. This doesn't limit your rights under the Australian Consumer Law.

Can my team use it?

Yes. One purchase covers use within one business, including its employees and contractors working for it. Please don't share the files outside your business, resell them or use them in services you sell to others. Advisers who want to use the skills across several client businesses should get in touch first.

What happens to the information I put in?

The skills run inside your own AI account. Nothing you enter or upload is sent to Rev-IQ. Your AI provider's terms, your plan's data settings and your organisation's policies apply, so remove anything you're not comfortable sharing.

How reliable are the outputs?

Each skill applies a fixed Rev-IQ framework, and several use a built-in calculator so the scoring and maths are consistent. The output is only as good as the information you give it, and AI can make mistakes. Check the figures and treat the output as a strong first draft, not a final answer. Results aren't guaranteed.
