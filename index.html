About Rev-IQ

Rev-IQ exists because most software businesses solve product problems well and commercial problems badly — not from a lack of intelligence, but from a lack of structure. We publish practical, experience-shaped thinking on what closes that gap.

The tone is deliberate: useful rather than noisy, clear rather than performative, and grounded in the belief that good commercial thinking should help leaders make better decisions — not sell them a program.

Approach

Rev-IQ is designed to foreground useful ideas over personality. The emphasis is on practical frameworks, grounded observations and thinking that helps leaders make better decisions about growth, retention and operating performance.
