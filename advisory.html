Diagnostic

Commercial diagnostic

For businesses that know something in the commercial engine isn't working and want the evidence before committing to a fix. It also gives an advisory board seat or fractional engagement a clear, evidence-based starting point.

Format: Fixed scope, agreed up front
What you leave with
  • An economic baseline built from your financial and CRM data
  • The real causes of churn and stalled growth, with the evidence
  • Clear positioning and a credible growth path
  • A prioritised 90-day plan with owners and dates
What this typically includes
  • Financial and CRM analysis
  • Stakeholder and customer interviews
  • A churn and retention review
  • A positioning review against the market you want to win
  • Dashboards for the numbers that matter

Advisory board

Advisory board seat

For founders and leadership teams with a plan who want experienced, independent oversight to keep execution on track. Joel joins your advisory board as the commercial voice, holding the team to what was agreed and helping make the calls that matter.

Format: Monthly, with pre-reading and follow-up
What you leave with
  • A standing monthly checkpoint on progress against the plan
  • Decisions made and owners named at every session
  • Early warning when execution starts to drift
  • Written follow-up with actions, owners and dates
What this typically includes
  • Pre-reading reviewed before each session
  • A monthly session on progress, the numbers and the next decisions
  • Commercial challenge on pipeline, pricing, retention and growth priorities
  • Input on board and investor readiness ahead of a raise or exit

Growth

Growth & GTM advisory

For businesses that need to build, fix or scale their go-to-market motion, from a first sales process to a pipeline that can actually be forecast.

Format: Advisory or hands-on implementation
What you leave with
  • A sharp definition of the target customer
  • One chosen go-to-market motion, with the reasoning written down
  • Qualification standards and pipeline stages the team actually uses
  • A measurement plan for pipeline, conversion and forecast accuracy
What this typically includes
  • Diagnosing whether the model is founder-led, sales-led or product-led, and whether that's still the right call
  • Designing or repairing the sales process, qualification criteria and pipeline structure
  • Reviewing pricing and packaging against the current market and customer base
  • Working alongside your existing sales and marketing leaders

Retention

Retention & customer journey advisory

For businesses where the front door works but the back end leaks: onboarding that varies by who's running it, customer success with no early warning system, or churn no one saw coming.

Format: Advisory or hands-on implementation
What you leave with
  • A customer journey map from signed deal to renewal, with owners at each stage
  • A standard onboarding scope
  • A working customer health model
  • Support tiers and response targets the team can meet
What this typically includes
  • Mapping the customer journey from signed deal through renewal
  • Designing a standard onboarding scope and implementation plan
  • Building a health-score model and a customer success operating rhythm
  • Setting support tiers and SLAs that match the size of the customer base

Sessions

Workshops, speaking & leadership sessions

For leadership teams, boards or events that want a grounded, commercially literate perspective on growth, retention and operating performance, delivered as a working session that ends in decisions.

  • Follow-up actions with dates, written up and shared afterwards

  • Leadership team workshops on a specific commercial problem: pipeline, pricing, retention, operating rhythm

  • Conference or event speaking on technology business growth, retention and revenue operations
